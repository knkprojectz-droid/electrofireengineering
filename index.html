Our Services

From engineered design to seamless execution, we build fire protection solutions that safeguard people, assets and operations.

Fire Hydrant Systems

Complete design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of fire hydrant systems including pumps, piping, hydrant valves, landing valves, hose reels and accessories.

Fire Alarm Systems

Supply and installation of addressable and conventional fire alarm systems including fire alarm panels, smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual call points, hooters and notification devices.

Fire Suppression Systems

Specialized fire suppression systems for critical areas such as electrical rooms, server rooms, transformer rooms and high-risk industrial applications.

Fire Extinguishers

Supply, installation, inspection, refilling and maintenance of portable fire extinguishers suitable for different classes of fire hazards.

Turnkey Engineering Projects

Comprehensive end-to-end engineering, procurement, and construction services for your fire protection needs — from concept to commissioning.
